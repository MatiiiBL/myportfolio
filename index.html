Hardware · SystemVerilog

Pulse

A RISC-V SoC with an INT8 systolic-array NPU.

A 5-stage pipelined RV32IM CPU driving an 8×8 INT8 systolic-array NPU with its own DMA, checked instruction by instruction against a C++ reference simulator. It meets timing at 40 MHz on an ECP5 FPGA (place and route; not yet run on a board).

Robotics · C++20

Apex

An autonomous Formula Student Driverless stack, and the simulator it races in.

In simulation, on a track it has never seen, lap 1 maps the cones with LiDAR, camera and EKF-SLAM; laps 2–10 race a minimum-curvature raceline with model predictive control. Every algorithm is written from scratch, down to the QP solvers.
